Mathematical Formulation (SIH26137)

1. Problem Definition (CVRP)

Given a depot \(0\), delivery customers \(C = \{1,\dots,n\}\), and homogeneous vehicles of capacity \(Q\), each customer has demand \(q_i\). Every stop is visited exactly once, each tour starts & ends at the depot, and vehicle load never exceeds \(Q\).

2. Traffic-Weighted Road Cost

cij = dij · (1 + γ · wij)

Where \(d_{ij}\) is the shortest-path distance (km) and \(w_{ij} \in [0,1]\) is simulated congestion intensity along edge \((i,j)\) weighted by factor \(\gamma = 0.85\).

3. Quantum Particle Swarm Optimization (QPSO)

Particles move in continuous search space \([0,1]^n\) with random-key order encoding. Mean best attractor \(mbest\) and local attractor \(p\) drive the wave equation:

xi(t+1) = pi ± β · |mbest - xi(t)| · ln(1 / u)

Where \(u \sim U(0,1)\) and \(\beta\) decays from \(1.0 \to 0.5\) over iterations to transition from quantum exploration to fine local exploitation.
